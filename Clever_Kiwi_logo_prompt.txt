Clever Kiwi logo prompt

Create a refined, memorable logo for “CyberKiwi,” a brand that turns cybersecurity into clear, manageable actions for small businesses and independent professionals.

Develop an elegant continuous-line kiwi bird symbol. Subtly suggest the letters C and K through the rounded body, long beak, and leg geometry. The kiwi must be recognizable at first glance; discovering the initials should be a secondary delight. Give the bird a grounded stance and an intelligent, approachable character.

Use a consistent, moderately bold line weight, smooth curves, restrained geometry, and carefully balanced negative space. Pair the symbol with a highly readable sans-serif wordmark spelled exactly “CyberKiwi.” Use deep forest green (#164B3B) on warm ivory (#F6F3EA).

Present one polished logo with generous whitespace. Keep the identity versatile enough for education, consulting, and future digital tools. Ensure it reproduces clearly in one color and at small sizes. Avoid forced letter shapes, tangled lines, fragile strokes, ornamental typography, gradients, shadows, 3D effects, and mockups.
